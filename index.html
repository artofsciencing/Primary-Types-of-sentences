🔑 Key Terms to Know

Independent Clause (IC) Dependent Clause (DC) Coordinating Conj. (FANBOYS) Subordinating Conj.

💡 IC = a complete thought that can stand alone  |  DC = incomplete — needs an IC to make sense

🔴 TYPE 1

Simple Sentence

Contains ONE independent clause. It has a subject and a verb, and expresses a complete thought. It may have phrases, but no other clauses.
📐 Formula: [ Independent Clause ]
  • 🐶 The dog barks.
  • ☀️ The sun rises in the east.
  • 🏃 She runs every morning.
  • 📚 Tom loves reading books.
  • 🐱 The cat sat on the mat.
  • 🎸 My brother plays the guitar beautifully.
  • 🍕 We ate pizza for dinner last night.
  • 🌧️ It rained heavily throughout the afternoon.
  • ✏️ The teacher wrote the answer on the board.
  • 🚀 The rocket launched into the sky.
🟡 TYPE 2

Compound Sentence

Contains TWO or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So) or a semicolon.
📐 Formula: [ IC ] + FANBOYS / ; + [ IC ]
  • ☀️🌧️ The sun was shining, but it was still cold outside.
  • 🎮📺 I wanted to play, but my homework was not done.
  • 🐱🐶 She likes cats, and he likes dogs.
  • 🍎🍊 You can have the apple, or you can have the orange.
  • 🏃🛋️ He ran fast, yet he missed the bus.
  • 💤📖 I was tired, so I went to bed early.
  • ⚽🏏 Maya loves football; her brother prefers cricket.
  • 📝✅ She studied hard, for the exam was very important.
  • 🎵🤫 He wanted to sing, but he did not know the words.
  • 🌊🏊 The waves were strong, nor did the swimmers give up.
🟢 TYPE 3

Complex Sentence

Contains ONE independent clause + ONE or more dependent clauses joined by a subordinating conjunction (because, although, when, if, since, after, before, while, unless, until…)
📐 Formula: [ IC ] + Sub.Conj. + [ DC ] OR Sub.Conj. + [ DC ] , [ IC ]
  • ☔ She carried an umbrella because it was raining.
  • 😴 I went to bed after I finished my homework.
  • 🎉 Although he was tired, he attended the party.
  • 🦁 The children hid when the lion roared.
  • 📱 I will call you as soon as I reach home.
  • 🏆 Since she trained every day, she won the championship.
  • ❄️ We stayed indoors until the storm passed.
  • 🚪 Unless you hurry, we will miss the show.
  • 📖 He reads books whenever he has free time.
  • ✈️ Before she boarded the plane, she called her parents.
🔵 TYPE 4

Compound-Complex Sentence

Contains TWO or more independent clauses AND at least ONE dependent clause. It combines the features of both compound and complex sentences.
📐 Formula: [ IC ] + FANBOYS + [ IC ] + Sub.Conj. + [ DC ]
  • 🌧️🏠 I stayed home, and I read a book because it was raining.
  • 📝🎉 She studied hard, so she passed the exam although it was difficult.
  • 🐕🏡 After we ate dinner, Tom washed the dishes, and Sara swept the floor.
  • ☀️🌊 The weather was perfect, so we went to the beach although we had a lot of work.
  • 🎓🏆 Because she practised daily, she became the best, and everyone admired her.
  • 🐦🌺 The birds sang while the sun rose, and the flowers bloomed in the garden.
  • 📱💬 I called him, but he did not answer because he was asleep.
  • 🎭📰 When the play ended, the audience cheered, and the actors took a bow.
  • 🏔️🥾 We reached the summit, and we celebrated because we had trained for months.
  • 🍲🌶️ The soup was delicious although it was spicy, so everyone asked for seconds.
